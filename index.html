Waffle math

Recipes serve four; brunch serves whoever shows up. Waffle math scales the batter honestly - whole eggs first, everything else follows - then tells you how long the iron will be tied up and when to start mixing. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Batter for a crowd - servings and style become eggs, flour, liquid and butter, scaled around whole eggs with the drift shown.
  • Iron shift - servings, style and iron count become piece count, rounds and wall-clock minutes.
  • Rest and hold - style and minutes until serving become the verdict: too soon, mix now, or too early.

Labeled norms

Per-serving ratios by style - buttermilk waffle 60 g flour / 95 g liquid / half an egg / 15 g butter, one piece, 4 min on the iron, rest 10 min, hold 30; belgian 70/100/three-quarter egg/20, one piece, 5 min, rest 30, hold 20; crepe 40/100/half an egg/8, three pieces, 2 min each, rest 60, hold 120; pancake 60/90/half an egg/10, three pieces, 3 min each, rest 5, hold 45. Egg weight 50 g. These are common kitchen norms, not a recipe - your iron, your batter and your taste win.
